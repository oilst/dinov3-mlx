| 5 All sizes | All model sizes in fp32, fp16 and bf16 | SwiGLU, model scaling, numeric range of the tokens | `specs/05-all-sizes/` | done (2026-11-07). Path D4-Y (the user's yes of 2026-11-07). On mlx, ViT-S/16 to ViT-H+/16 pass parity in 282 of 300 configurations (5 model sizes × 5 dtype modes × 12). The other 18 are the expected fp16 overflow of ViT-L/16 (12) and ViT-H+/16 (6) in fp16-cast, as on torch-mps. ViT-7B/16: the streamed fp32 reference holds the fp32 weights of one block at a time (peak 1.75 GB against a need of 4.82 GB; 26.86 GB for the whole model). It equals the five full-model reference files bit for bit (largest difference 0.0). Against it, the 48 mlx parity rows of ViT-7B/16 in the four fp16 and bf16 dtype modes pass, and the 24 torch-mps rows in fp16-cast and bf16-cast pass (largest share of a threshold budget: 43.9 %). The run rule refuses its 12 fp32 rows on mlx, and its 36 torch-mps rows in fp32, fp16-autocast and bf16-autocast (fp32 weights). Load peak of ViT-7B/16 in bf16: 13.5 GB. P2 paired ratio mlx / torch-mps: about 1 in the cast modes, 1.09 to 1.16 in fp32 and the autocast modes (both runs). AC13 is partial: the half SDPA kernel uses relaxed precision (F3). ADR 0007 and ADR 0008. |
| 6 Speed | Compiled graphs, batch sizes, input resolutions, quantized weights | Attention cost at high input resolution | `specs/06-speed/` | done (2026-11-08). Compiled forward pass on mlx, torch.compile rows on torch-mps, paired groups for every configuration. Paired verdicts mlx / torch-mps (pooled median of two runs, frame and patch output): P1 fp32 1.067 and 1.068 above 1; P1 fp16 0.867 and 0.900, bf16 0.878 and 0.881 not above 1; P2 fp32 1.092, fp16 1.032 and 1.033, bf16 1.013 (inside the noise) above 1. Matrix: 324 cells, 306 with a ratio: 140 above 1, 166 not above 1; 18 ViT-7B/16 fp32 cells empty (ADR 0008). In a half dtype both backends reach about the same largest matmul rate, and this rate grows with the model size: 28.0 TFLOPS (mlx) and 29.9 TFLOPS (torch-mps) at ViT-L/16, 32.3 and 33.0 at ViT-H+/16, 32.9 and 35.0 at ViT-7B/16; in fp32 both stay below 10.4 TFLOPS (Section 7 of `verify.html`). So mlx is at about the speed of torch.compile in the half dtypes. One paired round does not decide the cells near 1 (V17). Quantized weights: 8 bits valid in 72 of 72 rows, 4 bits not valid in 72 of 72 rows; 8 bits save memory, not time. Verify: 18 pass, 8 partial, 0 fail before the fixes; 20 pass, 8 partial, 0 fail after the Learn and Close stages; 21 pass, 7 partial, 0 fail now. AC25 now passes: the default session of 2026-11-08 at about 04:35 gave 946 passed, 0 failed in 49.6 s. AC1 and AC15 are partial only because the user has not confirmed deviations 1 and 2. ADR 0009, 0010 and 0011. |
| 6b Prior-art comparison | Measure the related public repos (MLX ports of DINOv2 and DINOv3, Core ML exports, mlx-image) with our harness: frame throughput and parity at the same model size, input resolution and batch size | Which parts of the backbone each port changes, and what that costs in parity | `specs/06b-prior-art/` | done (2026-11-08). Python ports C1 and C2 (J5, after the user's yes to Q1 on 2026-11-08, ADR 0013): ours is faster in all 8 cells (ratio ours ÷ port 1.16 to 1.53, pooled median of two runs of 5 paired rounds each; spread between runs 0.017 or less); in fp32 both ports miss the fp32 thresholds narrowly (patch p01 0.99985 and 0.99988), in bf16 both pass, and C1 gives NaN in fp16. Core ML exports C7 to C9 against our compiled forward pass, two runs of 5 paired rounds (ADR 0012): FP32 exports slower than ours (prior-art ratio 1.05 to 1.09; 1.53 at P1 with batch size 8, where an export makes 8 calls); FP16 exports equal or faster (ViT-B/16 448 px 0.98 to 0.99, inside the noise; ViT-S/16 512 px 0.93 to 0.94; DINOv2 at 1374 tokens 0.85 to 0.87). With float input the FP32 and FP16 DINOv3 exports (E1, E3 to E6) reproduce the reference output; the INT8 export E2 does not (patch p01 0.998562: fail, report only). The 8-bit image input costs 0.001 of patch 1st percentile. Port changes in our code: the bf16 RoPE of C1 and C2 fails the fp32 thresholds (patch p01 0.99953 ViT-S/16, 0.99838 ViT-H+/16); the unfused attention costs 10 to 24 % of the throughput. Verify: 10 of 12 at Verify, 12 of 12 after the fixes; a separate agent checked the fixes on 2026-11-08 (verify.html Section 7: 12 of 12). AC9 after J5: a separate agent checked it (Section 8, pass); its 5 low findings V11 to V15 are fixed (Section 9, 2026-11-09). |
| 6c Neural Engine | Explore Core ML on the Apple Neural Engine, alone and in parallel with the GPU, against mlx | How the Neural Engine runs attention and the feed-forward network, and its numeric range in fp16 | `specs/06c-neural-engine/` | done (2026-11-08), except AC4: Neural Engine parity fails for 13 of 15 rows (V1 of `verify.html`, high); the user decides the follow-up. Verify: 9 pass, 2 partial, 1 fail (AC4). |
