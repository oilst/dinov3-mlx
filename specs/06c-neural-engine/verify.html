1 Summary

This page checks the 12 acceptance criteria of specs/06c-neural-engine/plan.html (Section 8, Table 6, with the amendment of 2026-11-08) against the build. The agent did not build the step. It ran ruff, the default session, the slow session of tests/test_coreml.py and the Verify subset of plan Section 3.7, one job at a time under the GPU lock. It changed no code. Its new result lines (run 2) are in results/coreml.jsonl. On request of the orchestrator (free disk near 6 GB) it stopped the extra compute-plan job v8 after 11 of 26 models; the other 15 rows are «skipped: disk». All jobs of the Verify subset ran.

Result: 9 rows pass, 2 rows are partial, 1 row fails.

  • The rewrite works on the GPU, not on the Neural Engine. With rewrite version ne2 (score scale, residual-stream scale, ε / r²) all 90 rows on CPU_AND_GPU and ALL pass the fp16 thresholds (smallest frame cosine 0.99999). On CPU_AND_NE only 2 of 45 rows pass: ViT-S/16 and ViT-S+/16 at 224 px. The other 43 rows give finite but wrong tokens (frame cosine −0.05 to 0.07). AC4 fails (finding V1, high).
  • The cause is a second fault, not F1. The block probe (Q3) shows the first wrong tensor at the input of block 1, the output of the embeddings (cosine 0.31 for ViT-L/16 at 224 px, 0.19 for ViT-S/16 at 448 px), with the right largest value. The two passing packages keep the input cast, the patch convolution and the first attention on the CPU (compute plan: 46 ops on the CPU). Every package whose first ops run on the Neural Engine is wrong. A matmul patch embedding and an fp16 input did not change this.
  • The Neural Engine adds throughput at P1. The two-stream run at P1, batch size 8, gives a stream ratio of 1.257 (build) and 1.312 (Verify): the Neural Engine adds about 650 img/s to about 1,900 img/s of mlx, and mlx keeps 0.93 to 0.99 of its throughput. The P1 package is valid. At P2 the stream ratio is 0.934 and 0.981, and the Neural Engine output is wrong (Section 7).
  • Paired runs. No Core ML side is above mlx at batch size 8 (pooled: coreml-gpu 0.969 at P1, 0.790 at P2; coreml-all 0.983 and 0.739; coreml-ne 0.392 and 0.143). Only coreml-ne at P1, batch size 1 is above mlx (1.016, inside the noise). The two runs agree within 0.084.
  • Findings. Section 10 gives 10 findings: 1 high, 3 medium, 6 low. The high finding V1 needs a decision of the user: a follow-up step that splits the package into an embeddings part off the Neural Engine and a blocks part on it.

Reasons for the values below 1.05 (P2).

  1. The Neural Engine adds little: 2.5 to 2.8 img/s against 12 to 21 img/s of mlx. ViT-H+/16 at 512 px runs at 2.8 img/s on the Neural Engine, 0.14 to 0.17 of mlx.
  2. mlx slows down when the Neural Engine runs: in «both» mlx keeps only 0.77 to 0.86 of its throughput alone. The loss of mlx (2.5 to 4.6 img/s) is as large as the gain of the Neural Engine or larger. Both units share the memory bandwidth, and the Core ML call uses the CPU for the ops off the Neural Engine and for the transfer of the 1,029 × 1,280 fp32 output. The agent did not measure which part dominates.
  3. The Neural Engine output at P2 is wrong (frame −0.03 to 0.32 for the three images, V1). The streams plan falls back to batch size 1 because no coreml-ne matrix row of ViT-H+/16 at 512 px is valid. So the P2 throughput of the Neural Engine is not useful throughput, also where the ratio were above 1.
