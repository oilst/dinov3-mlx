2.1 Reasons of the cells below 0.97 (AC4)

The prior-art ratio of a cell is our patch throughput divided by the export throughput. Our patch time has two parts: the device time (the compiled forward pass to the end of mx.eval) and the move of the patch embeddings to the host. The device ratio is our device throughput divided by the export throughput. The transfer share is 1 − patch throughput ÷ device throughput of ours.
